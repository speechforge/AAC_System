What should I listen for?

You do not need to speak or understand these languages fluently. No specialist knowledge of the language is required. Rate each sample only on its overall speech naturalness: how human-like and fluent the voice sounds, with natural rhythm, intonation and pronunciation. Do not judge background noise or how clean the recording is, and you do not need to understand the words. Use 1 for very unnatural, robotic speech and 5 for very natural, human-like speech.
