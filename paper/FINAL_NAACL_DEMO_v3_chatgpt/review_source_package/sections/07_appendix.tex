\section{Scoring and Reproducibility Details}
\label{app:scorer}
The archived scorer matches lexical concept forms and stems, then checks polarity, act markers, additions, and off-task patterns.
A negator in a valid user refusal is correct; a refusal by the model to generate text is off-task.
The scorer-v1 Gujarati feeling-act gap explains all eight gold-field template failures.
Scorer v2 adds or repairs multilingual forms, perspective checks, negative-scope checks, and script checks; scores under it are reported only with that label.
Neither version is an independent semantic reference.

The repaired regression uses Qwen revision beginning 7ae5576, half precision, PyTorch 2.11.0, and Transformers 5.14.1 on a TITAN Xp.
English seed 11 and greedy Indic decoding are kept fixed.
Archived and repaired candidate streams remain separate, and the gate/retry/template policy is replayed over saved candidates.
The suffix-trie prediction comparison uses separate source-conversation partitions; the Hindi dialogues are translated synthetic conversations.
The native-review dependency is retained for the prepared 120-scenario evaluation rather than resolved by the lexical scorer.
