# Indic AAC Studio: revision story

**Thesis.** Explicit concept, polarity, and act checks improve the recorded ungated cascade on an Indic AAC grid, but template realization remains stronger under the archived lexical scorer; the repaired workflow is supported by regression evidence, not a validated generalization or usability claim.

**Assumptions and boundaries.** English is a reference-language condition in Figure 4, not a different generation algorithm. Hindi, Gujarati, and Marathi are the proposed Indic-language extensions of the same policy. The archived scorer-v1 comparison remains the main paired result; scorer-v2 regression is separately labelled. No scores from the draft 120-item holdout enter the manuscript. No human preference, real speech quality, or public-release claim is made.

**Narrative arc.** We wanted to learn whether a sentence generator can respect a short symbol selection. We compared the same candidates before and after the gate/retry/template policy, then audited interface and scorer errors. We learned that lexical preservation improves but acceptance does not guarantee meaning or naturalness. Repairs introduce separate act/polarity controls, multilingual checking, checked templates, clarification, and a literal preview.

**Novelty type and delta.** Application and diagnostic evaluation: explicit checks and fallback consequences for Hindi, Gujarati, and Marathi symbol selections. Compansion already expands telegraphic input; Karmakar and Sinha include Bengali symbol-to-text; Jellow already supports the three target Indic languages. No first-multilingual-AAC claim.

**Three contributions.** (1) A concept-ledger and acceptance policy with checked fallback. (2) A four-language interaction with distinct sentence, clarification, and preview outcomes. (3) A paired archived evaluation, failure audit, and separately labelled repair regression.

**Claims to avoid.** Semantic guarantees, hybrid superiority to templates, native-speaker preference, AAC usability, four-language audible speech, publicly verified demo availability, Gujarati/Marathi predictor scores, holdout performance before native gold review.
