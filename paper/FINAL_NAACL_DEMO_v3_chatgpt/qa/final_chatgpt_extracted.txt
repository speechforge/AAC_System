Ethics Statement                                                Hubert Siuzdak. 2023. Vocos: Closing the gap between

An AAC system can misattribute needs, feelings,                   high-quality audio synthesis. arXiv preprint.
or consent to its user. The interface should ex-
pose returned text and its route, preserve user con-
trol, and avoid automatically speaking ambiguous
or unchecked content. Neither a lexical score nor
a checker verdict is a clinical safety certificate. No
AAC users or native raters participated in the re-
ported results; the prepared rating materials do not
constitute a completed study. Future participant
studies require appropriate consent, review, and
accessible procedures. Reference voices must be
used only with explicit permission, and recordings
or personal vocabulary should not be released with-
out consent.

   Clarification is tested separately from sentence
success. In the recorded user-choice probe, the
user selects an act for an ambiguous word before
the system continues; the act choice can still lead
to a lexical match with the wrong sense. For ex-
ample, a Gujarati feeling interpretation of an am-
biguous label yields હું ખરાબ છું., which can mean
“I am bad” rather than feeling unwell. The gate’s
lexical match does not settle that distinction. An
unavailable server yields a labelled literal preview;
no candidate is invented to fill that failure.
