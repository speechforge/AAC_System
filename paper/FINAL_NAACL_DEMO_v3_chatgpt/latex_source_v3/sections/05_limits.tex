\section{Limitations and Availability}
\label{sec:limits}
The lexical evaluation covers short selections from a closed board and is not native-speaker adjudicated.
The same authoring process supplied rules, scenarios, and scoring lexicons, leaving a risk of correlated assumptions.
English generation is sampled, while the Indic path is greedy; regression success on the development-informing grid does not establish reliability on new messages.
Neither a target-user study nor a completed native-speaker preference study supports a claim about communication effectiveness or naturalness.
Hindi prediction dialogues are machine translations, and Gujarati and Marathi lack independent predictor evaluations.

The finite gate can accept an inappropriate sense or reject a valid construction, and its template frames cover only part of the user's possible vocabulary.
Clarification and literal preview make such boundaries visible but can increase interaction burden.
Real speech quality, target-locale browser voices, accessibility with screen readers and switch scanning, and full offline operation remain unverified.
The current evidence supports a research prototype and an error-analysis method, not unattended use for consequential communication.

The research package contains the local sources, recorded outputs, evaluation materials, and interface captures.
A publicly accessible installable package, hosted demo, and narrated video have not been verified.
The first-party release license is not established in the inspected materials, so we do not claim an open-source release.
Third-party assets retain their terms: Qwen uses Apache-2.0, IndicF5 and Vocos use MIT, and the bundled Noto fonts use the SIL Open Font License.
IndicF5 additionally requires access approval and permission for any cloned voice.

\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}
\sys{} makes concept, polarity, and act checks explicit in a four-language symbol-realization workflow.
The archived gate, retry, and template policy improves its ungated cascade on 175 scenarios while remaining behind the template baseline.
Auditing identifies interface errors, accepted model failures, and Gujarati scorer gaps; repairs add separate act control, multilingual checking, checked fallback, clarification, and literal previews.
Their regression success does not establish an advantage in naturalness or usability.
Independent native-speaker validation and real speech measurements are needed to determine what generated phrasing adds beyond the template.
