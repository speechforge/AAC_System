# Paper story — ChatGPT Version 3

Thesis: Explicit concept, polarity, and act checks with retry and fallback reduce the archived cascade's lexical errors in four languages, but do not establish an advantage over matched templates; the revised policy is supported only by labelled regression evidence.

Novelty type: System-design and evaluation contribution.
Novelty keywords: concept, polarity, act, fallback, gate

Territory: Symbol selections express a person's message, and grammatical realization adds material.
Niche: Fluent candidates can alter concepts, polarity, act, or speaker; lexical gates and scorers can also miss language-specific constructions.
Occupy: Make the acceptance policy explicit, diagnose its failures, distinguish UI/generator/gate/template/scorer, and document checked fallbacks and clarification.

Contributions: inspectable policy; paired four-language audit; evidence-linked repair and representative examples.
Claims avoided: first Indic AAC; native-judged faithfulness; superior naturalness; AAC usability gains; audible speech; public release; independent generalization from regression perfection.

Arc: We wanted to understand what the full acceptance policy prevents and what it misses. We replayed recorded candidates and audited their routes. The archived policy improves its own cascade while trailing a template. The repair fixes known paths on the regression set, and fresh human-validated evaluation remains open.
